## Persona 1 — The Paranoid Security Researcher

"Re-enabling `allUsers` was a necessary evil for the iframe, and I'm glad you stuck to your guns on the internal token validation. However, your `validate_google_token` function in `main.py` is a synchronous block inside your WebSocket handler's setup—if the Google `tokeninfo` endpoint is slow, you're tying up your worker. Also, I noticed your `main.py` still has a few `print` statements—if those ever leak a trace ID or a session detail into a public log, I'll be back."

**Gaps:**
| Severity | Problem | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | `main.py` L618: `print` statements are used for logging. | Switch to `logging.getLogger("uvicorn")` for structured, leveled logging. |
| **Minor** | `main.py` L615: CSP `script-src` includes `unsafe-eval`. | Audit the GIS library dependency to see if `unsafe-eval` can be removed via a newer SDK version. |

## Persona 2 — The Impatient UX Designer

"The 'Stage occupied' error is great, but it's just text. If I'm in the middle of a meeting, I might miss it. And while the 'Open Export' button is perfect, the 'Export' button in the Transcript section is still a plain text toggle. It feels like a settings switch, not a 'Save' action. Also, why do I have to click 'Connect' every time? Can't it remember my session for the duration of the meeting?"

**Gaps:**
| Severity | Problem | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | `index.tsx`: Export button lacks an icon. | Add a 'download' or 'drive' icon to the Export button to distinguish it from toggles. |
| **Minor** | `index.tsx`: No persistence of the 'Connect' state. | Use `sessionStorage` to keep the user 'Connected' if they refresh the side panel during the same meeting. |

There's no 'Explain why this document wasn't found' feature."

**Gaps:**
| Severity | Problem | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Significant** | README vs Reality: The 'Workspace Agent' capabilities aren't fully documented for the end-user. | Add a 'What can I say?' tooltip or help section in the UI showing sample voice commands. |

**Gaps:**
| Severity | Problem | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|
